FAB ACADEMY FINAL PROJECT

VR HUB

Smart Mobile VR Storage, Charging & Management Station

A digitally fabricated classroom system designed to securely store, organize, charge, monitor, and manage ten virtual-reality headsets.

01 — THE PROBLEM

Managing VR equipment in a classroom is difficult.

VR headsets require charging, cable management, secure storage, ventilation, organization, and a reliable way for teachers to know whether equipment is ready for use.

Traditional cabinets can store equipment, but they do not necessarily provide information about device availability or integrate storage, charging, monitoring, and classroom management into one system.

02 — THE IDEA

One mobile system for ten VR headsets.

VR Hub is designed as a modular mobile classroom station capable of supporting ten virtual-reality headsets and their controllers.

10 Headsets

Dedicated modular storage bays.

Charging

Organized charging and hidden cable routing.

Monitoring

Sensors determine whether equipment is docked.

Status LEDs

Visual feedback shows device or bay status.

Ventilation

Active cooling helps manage cabinet temperature.

Dashboard

A software interface displays system information.

03 — INITIAL SKETCHES

Early Concept Development

Before moving into CAD, I began the VR Hub project with hand-drawn sketches to explore the overall shape, storage layout, mobility, controller organization, display placement, and access to charging.

This early sketch shows the main ideas that would later guide the digital design: a compact mobile cabinet, a top display area, dedicated headset holders, controller storage inside the door, USB access, ventilation, and hidden wiring.

Original hand sketch of the mobile VR docking station concept.

04 — SYSTEMS

VR Hub is more than a cabinet.

Mechanical

CNC-cut cabinet structure, shelves, doors, caster system, controller storage, ventilation, and modular headset bays.

Fabrication

CNC machining, laser cutting, 3D printing, and additional fabrication processes.

Electronics

Custom PCB, sensors, microcontroller, status LEDs, and cooling controls.

Software

Embedded programming, communication, device monitoring, and dashboard interface.
