ABOUT ME

Educator. Technologist. Maker.

My background combines computer science, technology, education, data, and hands-on learning.

Who I Am

My name is Kenneth Riggs. I work in education and technology at Belen Jesuit Preparatory School, where I help students and teachers explore computer science, digital fabrication, programming, and emerging technologies.

Much of my work takes place inside the Innovation Center, where students can move beyond simply learning concepts and actually design, prototype, test, and build.

Fab Academy gives me an opportunity to expand those skills and better understand the complete digital fabrication process.

WHY FAB ACADEMY

Why I am taking this course

My goal is to develop the ability to move from an idea to a complete physical and digital system.

I want to become more confident designing with CAD, fabricating parts, creating electronics, programming microcontrollers, integrating sensors, developing interfaces, and combining those pieces into functional products.

I also want to bring what I learn back into the classroom and create more opportunities for students to become builders instead of only consumers of technology.
