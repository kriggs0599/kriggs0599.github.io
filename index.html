ABOUT

Building skills that can be brought back into the classroom.

I work at the intersection of education, technology, computer science, and digital fabrication.

Through Fab Academy, I am developing deeper skills in CAD, fabrication, electronics, embedded programming, networking, interface design, and system integration.

My goal is not just to learn how to use fabrication tools. I want to understand how to design complete systems and use those skills to create stronger hands-on learning experiences.

FINAL PROJECT

VR Hub

Smart Mobile VR Storage, Charging & Management Station

VR Hub is a digitally fabricated classroom system designed to securely store, organize, charge, monitor, and manage ten virtual-reality headsets.

The project will combine CNC fabrication, 3D printing, electronics, sensors, embedded programming, networking, interface design, and system integration.
